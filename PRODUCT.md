# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People preparing for interviews across software, data, AI/ML, infrastructure, security,
quality, product, business analysis, and UX/product design roles.

## Product Purpose

The platform conducts realistic, adaptive mock interviews from a candidate's resume and
optional job description. Success means each candidate receives a role-appropriate interview
with meaningful practical exercises, evidence-backed scoring, and a useful study plan.

## Positioning

Role profiles, rather than a single SDE interview template, determine the stages, question
families, practical exercise, competencies, and report rubric for each interview.

## Operating Context

Candidates upload a PDF resume, optionally provide a job description, choose a target role and
experience level, complete three timed stages, and review a consolidated report.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Supported roles include Software Engineer, Data Analyst, Data Scientist, AI/ML Engineer,
  Data Engineer, DevOps/Cloud Engineer, Cybersecurity Analyst, QA/Test Engineer, Product
  Manager, Business Analyst, and UX/Product Designer.
- Stage A assesses role depth and experience, stage B assesses role-specific practical problem
  solving, and stage C assesses a role-relevant system, workflow, product, or case challenge.
- Software Engineer stage B uses executable coding problems and hidden tests.
- Other roles use oral practical cases and must not be forced into system design or coding.
- Resume extraction and LLM failures degrade to explicit heuristic or retryable behavior.
- Existing session and round identifiers remain backward compatible.

## Product Principles

- Role first: the selected role controls the interview, not a universal SDE template.
- Practical evidence over familiarity: evaluate decisions, assumptions, and outcomes.
- One consistent quality bar: every role receives depth, adaptive follow-ups, and evidence-backed scoring.
- Honest uncertainty: mark untested competencies and limitations instead of inventing confidence.
- Preserve candidate agency: allow clarification, skipping, and respectful topic transitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interview supports text input, speech-to-text, spoken questions, keyboard interaction, and
clear status/error states. Role selection must not assume a technical background.
